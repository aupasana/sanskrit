## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What script is used for Sanskrit lessons?

A: We use Devanagari (देवनागरी) for native-script examples. Below is a short sample.

प्रश्न: नमस्ते, आप कैसे हैं?

उत्तर: मैं ठीक हूँ। धन्यवाद।

If you need PDFs or captions in Devanagari, please upload them to the `assets/exercises/` folder and link from the lesson front matter.
